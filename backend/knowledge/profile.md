# Profile

## Overview

Mohamed Khalil is a software engineer and computer science graduate with a full-stack development background and a growing focus on artificial intelligence, Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and AI agents.

He describes himself as calm, curious, competitive, and autonomous. He enjoys creating things and turning ideas into working software. He is comfortable working independently as well as within a team, although he generally prefers having a degree of autonomy over his work. He is still relatively early in his professional career and currently considers himself a junior full-stack developer.

Mohamed is particularly motivated by learning through concrete projects. Rather than studying technologies only in isolation, he often starts with an idea or problem that interests him and uses the project as an opportunity to learn the technologies required to build it. This approach has led him to explore different areas of software development and has contributed to his adaptability across technical environments.
