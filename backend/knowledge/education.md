# Engineering Degree in Computer Science

## Engineering Degree Overview

The program progressively covered computer science fundamentals, programming, databases, systems, networks, web development, software engineering, artificial intelligence, and engineering and professional methodologies.

The first year established foundations in programming, algorithms, mathematics, databases, operating systems, computer architecture, networks, web development, and information systems. The second year introduced more advanced software engineering, artificial intelligence, image processing, network administration, embedded systems, real-time systems, software architecture, advanced databases, and web services. The third year focused on advanced engineering topics and included a specialization in Artificial Intelligence, with subjects such as Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Difficult Problem Solving, and Trustworthy AI.

The Engineering Degree also included professional and engineering-oriented activities such as project management, team management, research and development, entrepreneurship, business strategy, communication, and engineering projects.

It also introduced professional and organizational skills through project management, team management, communication, and business-related subjects.

## Engineering Year 2

During the second engineering year, from 2023 to 2024, the curriculum moved toward more advanced software engineering, systems, artificial intelligence, and engineering topics. Mohamed studied Artificial Intelligence, Image Processing and Vision, Network Administration, Embedded Systems, Software Engineering, Agile Methods, Dynamic Systems Modeling, Real-Time Systems, Visual Programming and Instrumentation, Operations Research, Advanced Database Systems, Web Services and Software Architecture, and Research and Development.

During the third engineering year, from 2024 to 2025, Mohamed specialized in Artificial Intelligence while continuing to study advanced computer science and engineering topics. The Artificial Intelligence specialization included Deep Learning, Difficult Problem Solving, and Trustworthy AI. This specialization provided more advanced exposure to machine learning and AI while connecting those subjects with broader engineering and software development concepts.

# Professional Bachelor's Degree — GLASRRI

## Professional Bachelor's Degree Overview

From September 2021 to June 2022, Mohamed completed a Professional Bachelor's Degree in Génie Logiciel et Administration Avancée des Systèmes et Réseaux Informatiques (GLASRRI) at the École Supérieure de Technologie in Casablanca, Morocco.

The degree followed his DUT in Computer Science and provided more advanced training in software engineering, programming, systems administration, networks, databases, enterprise software, distributed systems, and web development. It combined software development with advanced systems and network administration, giving Mohamed a broader understanding of both application development and infrastructure.

The software engineering curriculum covered Software Engineering, Software Architecture, Design Patterns, Object-Oriented Software Development, Agile Methodologies, Human-Computer Interaction (IHM), and software development methodologies. These subjects developed his understanding of how software applications are designed, structured, developed, and maintained.

## Java, Enterprise, Mobile, and Web Development

The Professional Bachelor's Degree included advanced Java and enterprise development topics such as Advanced Java, JEE (Java Enterprise Edition), RMI (Remote Method Invocation), Object-Oriented Programming, and distributed application concepts.

The program also covered Android and mobile application development, as well as web application development with Django. These subjects expanded Mohamed's experience beyond basic programming into enterprise software, distributed systems, mobile development, and web applications.

The curriculum also included Data Mining and Business Intelligence, providing exposure to data analysis and business-oriented information systems.

## Systems and Networks

A significant part of the GLASRRI degree focused on advanced systems and network administration. Mohamed studied Linux system administration, advanced systems administration, and advanced network administration.

The networking curriculum included CCNA 3, CCNA 4, and IPv6. This part of the degree complemented his software engineering studies by providing knowledge of the infrastructure and network environments in which software systems operate.

## Main Knowledge Developed During the Professional Bachelor's Degree

The Professional Bachelor's Degree developed knowledge in software engineering, software architecture, Design Patterns, object-oriented programming, Advanced Java, JEE, RMI, Android development, Django, Agile methodologies, Data Mining, Business Intelligence, Linux administration, systems administration, network administration, CCNA, IPv6, and Human-Computer Interaction.

# University Diploma of Technology — DUT in Computer Science

From September 2019 to June 2021, Mohamed completed a four-semester University Diploma of Technology (DUT) in Computer Science at the École Supérieure de Technologie in Casablanca, Morocco.

The second semester covered Management and Communication, Data Structures and Server-Side Web Development, Fundamentals of Systems and Networks, and Information Systems and Databases.

This semester consolidated his previous technical knowledge through software engineering and database programming while introducing practical projects and technical internships. These experiences contributed to the transition from academic fundamentals toward professional software development.

## Main Knowledge Developed During the DUT

The DUT established foundational knowledge in programming, algorithms, data structures, client-side web development, server-side web development, object-oriented programming, software engineering, database programming, database systems, information systems, computer architecture, systems fundamentals, computer networks, systems administration, network administration, database administration, mathematics, communication, technical projects, and technical internships.
